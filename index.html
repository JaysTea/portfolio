About Me

Hello! I'm Jason, a Singapore-based game designer/ programmer and a creator at heart. I'm a Year 3 Computer Science student in Interactive Media and Game Development at DigiPen Singapore, graduating in 2028. I started out in C++ engine development and I'm now expanding into level design. I led a 10-person team building our own engine, Hellmaker. Outside of code, I play music, draw, edit videos, tinker with my homelab, and am currently learning character design!

What I Do

Game Design

Form follows function. I design from the shoes of whoever lives in a space, so levels feel believable and players can lean on what they already know instead of being taught. When inspiration doesn't come, I go Similar yet Different: start from something that works, then push it into its own thing.

Software Programming

I started out in C++ engine development, leading a 10-person team to build our own engine, Hellmaker, while working on its architecture and gameplay systems. I've also built a Unity WebGL roguelike in C# for NTU's medical school, plus a Pixi.js prototype and a React website. Being both designer and programmer, I get to build the systems my designs need.
